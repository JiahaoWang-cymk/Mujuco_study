推测接触#

Speculative contacts

备注

本页由 AI（Claude Sonnet）根据 MuJoCo 官方英文文档（2026-11-16 版）翻译，仅供学习参考；如有出入，以英文原版为准。术语对照见资料包中的 术语表.md 。

推测接触（speculative contacts）用于防止快速运动的物体相互穿透。由于标准碰撞检测（collision detection）只在离散的时间步长（timestep）处检查 geom 的位姿，当 geom 在单步内的位移超过其厚度时，它们可能会相互 穿隧 （tunnel），即在两个时间步处都没有重叠，却从一侧穿到另一侧。对于包含高速运动刚体（body）、薄 geom 或较大时间步长的仿真，这一问题尤为突出。

Because standard collision detection only inspects geom poses at discrete timesteps, geoms can tunnel through each other whenever their displacement over a single step exceeds their thickness, passing from one side to the other without overlapping at either step. This is especially problematic for simulations with fast-moving bodies, thin geoms, or large timesteps.

推测接触的原理是 推测 在当前时间步与下一时间步之间本会发生的接触。该功能可通过 speculative 标志启用。推测接触的工作方式与普通接触相同，求解器（solver）对它们的处理也一视同仁。它们由以下流程产生：

Speculative contacts work by speculating contacts that would have occurred between the current and the next timestep. This feature can be enabled using the speculative flag. Speculative contacts work like normal contacts and are treated the same by the solver. They are produced by the following pipeline:

  1. 粗测阶段（broadphase）与球体过滤 扩展 sweep-and-prune 的 AABB 和包围球检测，以涵盖每个高速运动 geom 在该时间步内的线性运动和旋转运动。

    Broadphase and sphere filtering expands the sweep-and-prune AABBs and bounding-sphere tests to account for each fast-moving geom’s linear and rotational motion over the step.

  2. 保守推进 对候选 geom 对进行扫掠，确定撞击时间（time of impact，TOI）\(0 < t < 1\)，以占时间步长的比例表示。

    Conservative advancement sweeps candidate geom pairs to determine the time of impact (TOI) \(0 < t < 1\) as a fraction of the timestep.

  3. 视线射线投射 在两个 geom 之间发射一条射线，以排除被中间其他 geom 遮挡的推测接触。

    Line-of-sight raycasting casts a ray between the two geoms to exclude speculative contacts obscured by another geom in the middle.

  4. 有效接触距离 对求解器的参考加速度方程 (\(\ari = -b_i (J v)_i - k_i r_i\)) 求逆，计算出一个合成的接触距离 dist，使约束求解器（constraint solver）在该时间步内抵消相对法向速度的 \((1 - t)\) 部分，并在 \(t = 1\) 时使 geom 落在接触表面上。

    Effective contact distance inverts the solver’s reference acceleration equation (\(\ari = -b_i (J v)_i - k_i r_i\)) to compute a synthetic contact distance dist so that the constraint solver arrests \((1 - t)\) of the relative normal velocity over the step and lands the geom on the contact surface at \(t = 1\).

演示推测接触的示例模型见 model/speculative/。

Example models demonstrating speculative contacts can be found in model/speculative/.

启用推测接触会在每一步增加与场景相关的碰撞开销，该开销随单步行程超过自身厚度的 geom 数量，以及它们所扫掠经过的候选对数量而增长。与此同时，防止穿隧往往允许使用比离散碰撞检测所能容忍的更大的时间步长，从而降低总体的实际运行时间。

Enabling speculative contacts adds scene-dependent collision overhead per step, scaling with the number of geoms whose single-step travel exceeds their thickness and the candidate pairs they sweep across. At the same time, preventing tunneling often allows a larger timestep than discrete collision detection would tolerate, reducing overall wall-clock time.

备注

Note

推测扫掠仅使用步前速度 (mjData.qvel) 来外推 geom 的运动，而忽略加速度。这一方面是因为碰撞检测在位置阶段运行，早于力和加速度的计算；另一方面是因为在单步内，加速度贡献的位移为 \(O(\Delta t^2)\)，相比之下速度项为 \(O(\Delta t)\)。因此，时间步长 \(\Delta t\) 不能被任意增大：如果一个缓慢运动或静止的 geom 在单步内被很大的外加力、重力，或在约束求解过程中被另一个刚体推动而越过障碍物，其步前速度就无法预测这一运动，它仍然可能发生穿隧。

Speculative sweeps extrapolate geom motion using only pre-step velocities (mjData.qvel) and ignore acceleration. This is both because collision detection runs in the position stage before forces and accelerations are evaluated, and because acceleration contributes an \(O(\Delta t^2)\) displacement over a single step compared to the \(O(\Delta t)\) velocity term. Consequently, the timestep \(\Delta t\) cannot be made arbitrarily large: if a slow or stationary geom is accelerated across an obstacle within a single step by a large applied force, gravity, or another body pushing it during the constraint solve, its pre-step velocity will not predict the motion and it can still tunnel.

粗测阶段与球体过滤#

Broadphase and sphere filtering

为避免在缓慢运动的物体上产生开销，只有当 geom 表面上任一点在时间步长 \(\Delta t\) 内行进的最大距离超过其局部 AABB 半边长的最小尺寸时，该 geom 才被视为高速运动。给定步前线性位移 \(\Delta x = \Delta t \, v\) 和绕 geom 中心 \(c\) 的角位移 \(\Delta \omega = \Delta t \, \omega\)，geom 表面上的任一点 \(p\) 在该步内的位移为

To avoid overhead on slow-moving objects, a geom is only treated as fast-moving if the maximum distance traveled by any point on its surface over the timestep \(\Delta t\) exceeds the minimum dimension of its local AABB half-extents. Given pre-step linear displacement \(\Delta x = \Delta t \, v\) and angular displacement \(\Delta \omega = \Delta t \, \omega\) about the geom center \(c\), any point \(p\) on the geom surface moves over the step by
\[\Delta p = \Delta x + \Delta \omega \times (p - c) \]

由于 \(\|p - c\| \le r_{\text{bound}}\)，其中 \(r_{\text{bound}}\) 是 geom 的包围球半径，根据三角不等式，该表面行程以如下扫掠距离为上界

Since \(\|p - c\| \le r_{\text{bound}}\), where \(r_{\text{bound}}\) is the geom’s bounding-sphere radius, this surface travel is bounded above via the triangle inequality by the sweep distance
\[s = \|\Delta x\| + \|\Delta \omega\| \, r_{\text{bound}} \]

扫掠距离 \(s\) 小于其局部 AABB 半边长最小尺寸的 geom，在单步内不可能发生穿隧，因此使用标准的离散包围范围。

Geoms whose sweep distance \(s\) is smaller than the minimum dimension of their local AABB half-extents cannot tunnel in a single step and use standard discrete bounds.

扫掠包围球过滤器#

Swept bounding-sphere filter

对于每个候选 geom 对，包围球过滤器检验两个包围球在时间步长的任一比例 \(t \in [0, 1]\) 处是否可能重叠。记 \(p = x_2 - x_1\) 为初始中心间距，\(v = \Delta x_2 - \Delta x_1\) 为该步内的相对线性位移，则相对中心轨迹为 \(r(t) = p + t v\)。通过求导数 \(\frac{d}{dt}\|r(t)\|^2 = 2(p + t v) \cdot v = 0\) 的根 \(t^*\)，我们得到 \(\|r(t)\|^2\) 在 \([0, 1]\) 上的全局极小点 \(\tau\)：

For each candidate geom pair, the bounding-sphere filter tests whether the two bounding spheres can overlap at any fraction \(t \in [0, 1]\) of the timestep. Writing \(p = x_2 - x_1\) for the initial center separation and \(v = \Delta x_2 - \Delta x_1\) for the relative linear displacement over the step, the relative center trajectory is \(r(t) = p + t v\). We find the global minimizer \(\tau\) of \(\|r(t)\|^2\) on \([0, 1]\) by finding the root \(t^*\) of the derivative \(\frac{d}{dt}\|r(t)\|^2 = 2(p + t v) \cdot v = 0\):
\[\tau = \begin{cases} 0, & \phantom{0 \lt {}} t^* \le 0 \\ t^*, & 0 \lt t^* \lt 1 \\ 1, & 1 \le t^* \end{cases} \qquad \text{where } t^* = -\frac{p \cdot v}{\|v\|^2} \]

若 \(\|r(\tau)\| > r_{\text{bound},1} + r_{\text{bound},2} + \text{margin} + \text{gap}\)，则该对被剪除。当其中一个 geom 是单位法向量为 \(n\) 的平面时，过滤器检查端点 \(t \in \{0, 1\}\) 处的最小有符号距离，若 \(\min(d_0,\, d_0 + n \cdot \Delta x) > r_{\text{bound}} + \text{margin} + \text{gap}\)，则剪除该对。

The pair is pruned if \(\|r(\tau)\| > r_{\text{bound},1} + r_{\text{bound},2} + \text{margin} + \text{gap}\). When one of the geoms is a plane with unit normal \(n\), the filter checks the minimum signed distance at the endpoints \(t \in \{0, 1\}\), pruning the pair if \(\min(d_0,\, d_0 + n \cdot \Delta x) > r_{\text{bound}} + \text{margin} + \text{gap}\).

扫掠粗测阶段 AABB#

Swept broadphase AABBs

在 sweep-and-prune 期间 (选择)，每个 geom 的有向包围盒会被投影到场景协方差坐标系的三个主轴 \(\hat{u}_j\) 上。对于高速运动的 geom，沿 \(\hat{u}_j\) 的静态半投影首先按其旋转扫掠半径 \(\|\Delta \omega\| \, r_{\text{bound}}\) 膨胀，再与其包围球半径 \(r_{\text{bound}}\) 取交集（因为绕 geom 中心的旋转不可能把任何点移到 \(r_{\text{bound}}\) 之外）。随后，所得区间 \([\text{lo}_j, \text{hi}_j]\) 按线性位移投影 \(\Delta x \cdot \hat{u}_j\) 沿方向延伸：

During sweep-and-prune (Selection), each geom’s oriented bounding box is projected onto the three principal axes \(\hat{u}_j\) of the scene covariance frame. For a fast-moving geom, the static half-projection along \(\hat{u}_j\) is first inflated by its rotational sweep radius \(\|\Delta \omega\| \, r_{\text{bound}}\) and intersected with its bounding sphere radius \(r_{\text{bound}}\) (since rotation about the geom center cannot move any point outside \(r_{\text{bound}}\)). The resulting interval \([\text{lo}_j, \text{hi}_j]\) is then extended directionally by the linear displacement projection \(\Delta x \cdot \hat{u}_j\):
\[\big[\text{lo}_j + \min(0, \Delta x \cdot \hat{u}_j) - \text{margin},\; \text{hi}_j + \max(0, \Delta x \cdot \hat{u}_j) + \text{margin}\big] \]

由于每个刚体的包围体层次结构（bounding volume hierarchy，BVH）(mjModel.bvh_aabb) 是在编译时于刚体坐标系中静态预先计算的，对于包含高速运动 geom 的刚体对，会跳过中间阶段（mid-phase）的 BVH 树遍历，以免被扫掠的 geom 对被未膨胀的内部树节点剔除。

Because per-body bounding volume hierarchies (mjModel.bvh_aabb) are precomputed statically in the body frame at compile time, mid-phase BVH tree traversal is bypassed for body pairs containing a fast-moving geom so that swept geom pairs are not culled by uninflated internal tree nodes.

保守推进#

Conservative advancement

计算撞击时间有许多方法，概览可参见 Erin Catto 的 GDC 2013 演讲。在 MuJoCo 中，当离散精测阶段（narrow-phase）碰撞检测未发现有效的表面接触（dist < margin），且该对中至少有一个 geom 超过快速扫掠阈值时，我们使用一种单侧的 有向保守推进 算法来求撞击时间 \(t \in (0, 1)\)。

There are many approaches to computing the time of impact (see Erin Catto’s GDC 2013 presentation for an overview). In MuJoCo, when discrete narrowphase collision detection finds no active surface contact (dist < margin) and at least one geom in the pair exceeds the fast-sweep threshold, we use a unilateral, directed conservative advancement algorithm to find the time of impact \(t \in (0, 1)\).

在每次迭代中，GJK 在当前推进后的位姿下计算两个 geom 之间的最小距离 \(\text{dist}(t)\)、最近见证点（witness points）\((x_1(t), x_2(t))\)，以及分离单位法向量 \(n = (x_2(t) - x_1(t)) / \text{dist}(t)\) (由 geom 1 指向 geom 2)。根据分离轴定理（Separating Axis Theorem，SAT），在 \(x_1(t)\) 和 \(x_2(t)\) 处垂直于 \(n\) 的支撑平面，沿 \(n\) 方向约束了每一对点 \(p_1(t) \in \text{geom } 1\) 和 \(p_2(t) \in \text{geom } 2\)：

At each iteration, GJK evaluates the minimal distance \(\text{dist}(t)\) between the two geoms, closest witness points \((x_1(t), x_2(t))\), and separating unit normal \(n = (x_2(t) - x_1(t)) / \text{dist}(t)\) (pointing from geom 1 to geom 2) at the current advanced poses. By the Separating Axis Theorem (SAT), the supporting planes perpendicular to \(n\) at \(x_1(t)\) and \(x_2(t)\) bound every pair of points \(p_1(t) \in \text{geom } 1\) and \(p_2(t) \in \text{geom } 2\) along \(n\):
\[(p_2(t) - p_1(t)) \cdot n \;\ge\; (x_2(t) - x_1(t)) \cdot n \;=\; \text{dist}(t) \]

由于每个 geom 绕其中心 \(c_i\) 平移并旋转，步位移为 \((\Delta x_i, \Delta \omega_i)\)，geom \(i\) 上的任一点 \(p_i\) 关于 \(t\) 的速度为 \(\dot{p}_i = \Delta x_i + \Delta \omega_i \times (p_i - c_i)\)。由于 geom \(i\) 上的每个点都位于以 \(c_i\) 为中心、半径为 \(r_{\text{bound},i}\) 的包围球内 (\(\|p_i - c_i\| \le r_{\text{bound},i}\))，且 \(\|n\| = 1\)，每个旋转项都满足 \(\big|n \cdot (\Delta \omega_i \times (p_i - c_i))\big| \le \|\Delta \omega_i\| \, r_{\text{bound},i}\)。因此，将相对闭合速度 \(\dot{p}_1 - \dot{p}_2\) 投影到固定轴 \(n\) 上，就得到了两个 geom 上任意两点穿过分离平面的闭合速度的上界 \(v_{\text{bound}}\)：

As each geom translates and rotates about its center \(c_i\) with step displacements \((\Delta x_i, \Delta \omega_i)\), any point \(p_i\) on geom \(i\) moves with velocity \(\dot{p}_i = \Delta x_i + \Delta \omega_i \times (p_i - c_i)\) with respect to \(t\). Because every point on geom \(i\) lies within its bounding sphere of radius \(r_{\text{bound},i}\) centered at \(c_i\) (\(\|p_i - c_i\| \le r_{\text{bound},i}\)) and \(\|n\| = 1\), each rotational term satisfies \(\big|n \cdot (\Delta \omega_i \times (p_i - c_i))\big| \le \|\Delta \omega_i\| \, r_{\text{bound},i}\). Projecting the relative closing velocity \(\dot{p}_1 - \dot{p}_2\) onto the fixed axis \(n\) therefore gives an upper bound \(v_{\text{bound}}\) on how fast any two points on the geoms can close across the separating plane:
\[\begin{aligned} n \cdot (\dot{p}_1 - \dot{p}_2) &= n \cdot (\Delta x_1 - \Delta x_2) + n \cdot \big(\Delta \omega_1 \times (p_1 - c_1)\big) - n \cdot \big(\Delta \omega_2 \times (p_2 - c_2)\big) \\ &\le n \cdot (\Delta x_1 - \Delta x_2) + \|\Delta \omega_1\| \, r_{\text{bound},1} + \|\Delta \omega_2\| \, r_{\text{bound},2} \;\equiv\; v_{\text{bound}} \end{aligned} \]

与最坏情况下的各向同性扫掠 \(s_1 + s_2\) 不同——后者在所有方向上界定线性运动，当 geom 擦过表面时沿切向快速运动会使其停滞—— 将 \(\Delta x_1 - \Delta x_2\) 投影到 \(n\) 上会舍去平行于分离平面的切向线性运动。

Unlike the worst-case isotropic sweep \(s_1 + s_2\)—which bounds linear motion in all directions and stalls when a geom moves rapidly tangentially while grazing a surface—projecting \(\Delta x_1 - \Delta x_2\) onto \(n\) discards tangential linear motion parallel to the separating plane.

若 \(v_{\text{bound}} \le 0\)，则两个 geom 无法沿 \(n\) 闭合，搜索在无接触的情况下终止。否则，推进 \(\Delta t \ge 0\) 至多能使沿 \(n\) 的投影减少 \(\Delta t \, v_{\text{bound}}\)，因此 \(t + \Delta t\) 时的真实间距的下界为

If \(v_{\text{bound}} \le 0\), the geoms cannot close along \(n\) and the search terminates without contact. Otherwise, advancing by \(\Delta t \ge 0\) can reduce the projection along \(n\) by at most \(\Delta t \, v_{\text{bound}}\), so the true separation at \(t + \Delta t\) is bounded below by
\[\text{dist}(t + \Delta t) \;\ge\; \min_{p_1, p_2}\,(p_2(t + \Delta t) - p_1(t + \Delta t)) \cdot n \;\ge\; \text{dist}(t) - \Delta t \, v_{\text{bound}} \]

将该下界设为略高于 margin 的目标间隙 \(\text{dist}_{\text{target}} = \text{margin} + 0.02\,(\text{dist}(0) - \text{margin})\) (\(\text{dist}(t) - \Delta t \, v_{\text{bound}} = \text{dist}_{\text{target}}\))，即可得到保证安全的最大步长：

Setting this lower bound equal to a target clearance \(\text{dist}_{\text{target}} = \text{margin} + 0.02\,(\text{dist}(0) - \text{margin})\) just above margin (\(\text{dist}(t) - \Delta t \, v_{\text{bound}} = \text{dist}_{\text{target}}\)) yields the largest guaranteed-safe step:
\[\Delta t = \frac{\text{dist}(t) - \text{dist}_{\text{target}}}{v_{\text{bound}}}, \qquad t \leftarrow t + \Delta t \]

与 双侧推进 不同——后者可能越过而进入重叠状态，并借助有符号穿透（penetration）深度查询向前和向后两个方向步进—— 这种单侧推进严格从分离一侧 (\(\text{dist}(t) > \text{margin}\)) 逼近 \(\text{dist}_{\text{target}}\)，因此 GJK 总能返回定义明确的见证点和分离法向量，而无需调用 EPA。当启用 multiccd 时（或其中一个 geom 是平面时），多边形裁剪会在推进后的撞击位姿处提取至多四个接触点。

Unlike bilateral advancement—which can overshoot into overlap and step both forward and backward using signed penetration depth queries—this unilateral advancement approaches \(\text{dist}_{\text{target}}\) strictly from the separated side (\(\text{dist}(t) > \text{margin}\)), so GJK always returns well-defined witness points and a separating normal without invoking EPA. When multiccd is enabled (or one geom is a plane), polygon clipping extracts up to four contact points at the advanced impact pose.

视线射线投射#

Line-of-sight raycasting

在撞击时刻 \(t\) 找到的见证点 \(x_1(t)\) 和 \(x_2(t)\)，会从各 geom 推进后的局部坐标系映射回其 \(t = 0\) 时的世界位姿 \(x_1(0)\) 和 \(x_2(0)\)，并按各 geom 的扫掠比例 \(w_i = s_i / (s_1 + s_2)\) 插值，得到接触位置：

The witness points \(x_1(t)\) and \(x_2(t)\) found at impact time \(t\) are mapped from each geom’s advanced local frame back to its \(t = 0\) world pose \(x_1(0)\) and \(x_2(0)\), and interpolated by each geom’s sweep fraction \(w_i = s_i / (s_1 + s_2)\) to form the contact position:
\[x_{\text{contact}} = w_1 \, x_1(0) + w_2 \, x_2(0) \]

由于精测阶段对每个 geom 对独立求值，高速运动的 geom 可能会与位于中间障碍物 后方 的第二个物体产生推测接触（例如，一个下落的盒子与它即将撞击的薄桌面下方的地板产生推测接触）。为保证视线可达，会在 \(t = 0\) 时的见证点 \(x_1(0)\) 和 \(x_2(0)\) 之间投射一条射线段，并将其与所有与两个刚体兼容的其他可碰撞 geom 进行相交测试；若任何中间 geom 与该线段相交，则被遮挡的推测接触将被丢弃。

Because narrowphase evaluates each geom pair independently, a fast-moving geom could otherwise generate a speculative contact with a second object sitting behind an intervening obstacle (for example, a falling box registering a speculative contact with the floor underneath a thin tabletop it is about to strike). To enforce line-of-sight, a ray segment is cast between the \(t = 0\) witness points \(x_1(0)\) and \(x_2(0)\) and tested against all other collidable geoms compatible with both bodies; if any intervening geom intersects the segment, the occluded speculative contact is discarded.

有效接触距离#

Effective contact distance

由于推测接触是在 \(t = 0\) 时、即几何重叠发生之前创建的，其真实间距超过 margin。若把真实距离传给约束求解器，要么接触保持未激活状态，要么在以零穿透激活时，使参考加速度中的速度阻尼项

Because a speculative contact is created at \(t = 0\) before geometric overlap occurs, its true separation exceeds margin. Passing the true distance to the constraint solver would either leave the contact inactive or, if activated with zero penetration, cause the velocity damper in the reference acceleration
\[\ari = -b_i (J v)_i - k_i r_i \]

立即抵消 \(100\%\) 的接近速度——使刚体在 \(t = 0\) 时冻结在半空中。

to arrest \(100\%\) of the closing speed immediately—freezing the body in mid-air at \(t = 0\).

取而代之，碰撞检测会对该参考加速度方程求逆，解出有效接触距离 \(\text{dist} = \text{margin} - \delta\)，使得约束求解器在该时间步长内抵消入射法向速度 \(|v_n|\) 的 \((1 - t)\) 部分，留下剩余的 \(t\) 部分速度，在步末将 geom 带到接触表面上 (\(z_1 = z_0 + h v_1 = 0\))。

Instead, collision detection inverts this reference acceleration equation to solve for an effective contact distance \(\text{dist} = \text{margin} - \delta\) such that the constraint solver arrests \((1 - t)\) of the incoming normal speed \(|v_n|\) over the timestep, leaving the remaining fraction \(t\) of the velocity to carry the geom onto the contact surface at the end of the step (\(z_1 = z_0 + h v_1 = 0\)).

在标准欧拉（Euler）积分器（integrator）下，具有无量纲单步刚度（stiffness）\(k_0 = h^2 k\) 和阻尼 \(b_0 = h b\) (由 solref 和 solimp 导出) 的接触，会产生如下单步法向速度变化

Under the standard Euler integrator, a contact with dimensionless single-step stiffness \(k_0 = h^2 k\) and damping \(b_0 = h b\) (derived from solref and solimp) produces a single-step normal velocity change
\[h \ari = b_0 |v_n| + k_0 \frac{\delta}{h} \]

令 \(h \ari = (1 - t)|v_n|\) 并求解有效穿透深度 \(\delta\)，得到

Setting \(h \ari = (1 - t)|v_n|\) and solving for the effective penetration depth \(\delta\) yields
\[\delta = \frac{1 - t - b_0}{k_0} \, h |v_n|, \qquad \text{dist} = \text{margin} - \delta \]

在隐式 discrete 积分器下，弹簧阻尼器在步末位置处求值并除以隐式因子 \(1 + b_0 + k_0\)，分子 \(1 - t - b_0\) 被替换为 \(1 - t - t(b_0 + k_0)\)：

Under the implicit discrete integrator, where the spring-damper is evaluated at the end-of-step position and divided by the implicit factor \(1 + b_0 + k_0\), the numerator \(1 - t - b_0\) is replaced by \(1 - t - t(b_0 + k_0)\):
\[\delta_{\text{discrete}} = \frac{1 - t - t(b_0 + k_0)}{k_0} \, h |v_n| \]

注意，对于默认的临界阻尼接触 (\(b_0 = 1\))，\(1 - t - b_0 = -t < 0\)，因此 \(\delta < 0\) 且 \(\text{dist} > \text{margin}\)：弹簧项会抵着阻尼器向 回 拉 \(t |v_n|\)，使得在接近步中只去除 \((1 - t)|v_n|\)。即使 \(\text{dist} \ge \text{margin}\)，推测接触仍保持激活。

Notice that for default critically damped contacts (\(b_0 = 1\)), \(1 - t - b_0 = -t < 0\), so \(\delta < 0\) and \(\text{dist} > \text{margin}\): the spring term pulls back against the damper by \(t |v_n|\) so that only \((1 - t)|v_n|\) is removed during the approach step. Speculative contacts remain active even when \(\text{dist} \ge \text{margin}\).

建模注意事项#

Modeling notes

  • 弹性恢复： 标准推测接触将撞击分摊到两个步中——第一步抵消 \((1 - t)|v_n|\) 以落到表面上，下一步再处理表面接触。当通过直接设置负的 solref 来模拟近似弹性碰撞时（例如 \(b_0 \approx 2\)，使阻尼器在单步内将 \(v_n \to -v_n\) 反向，如 newton.xml 中所示），请将刚度设为零（例如 solref="0 -499"），并配合较硬的阻抗（impedance）(solimp="0.995 0.995 0.001")，这样推测位置弹簧项就不会在接近步中抵消弹性阻尼器（注意，零刚度不会为静止接触提供静态位置弹簧）。

    Elastic restitution: Standard speculative contacts split impact across two steps—arresting \((1 - t)|v_n|\) to land on the surface on the first step, and resolving surface contact on the next. When modeling nearly elastic collisions via direct negative solref (e.g. \(b_0 \approx 2\) so the damper reverses \(v_n \to -v_n\) in a single step, as in newton.xml), set the stiffness to zero (e.g. solref="0 -499") with a stiff impedance (solimp="0.995 0.995 0.001") so the speculative position spring term does not cancel out the elastic damper during the approach step (noting that zero stiffness provides no static position spring for resting contacts).

  • 单遍步前速度： 由于推测扫掠使用步前速度 (mjData.qvel)，被快速邻居撞击的静止中间刚体，只有在求解器运行之后才会获得其撞击后的速度。在多刚体撞击链（如牛顿摆）中，应在相邻刚体之间保留较小的间隙，或选择一个时间步长，使单步行程不会同时跨越两个间隙。

    Single-pass pre-step velocities: Because speculative sweeps use pre-step velocities (mjData.qvel), a stationary intermediate body struck by a fast neighbor acquires its post-impact velocity only after the solver runs. In multi-body impact chains (such as Newton’s cradle), keep a small clearance between adjacent bodies or choose a timestep where a single step’s travel does not jump across two gaps at once.
